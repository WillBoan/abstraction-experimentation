**What moved since 2026-07-07.** No experiment — a **synthesis**. The frame sharpened around a single research object it had been circling without naming: **the search-cost graph** (axis 2 below).

Three shifts:

1. a **thesis** — the study _is_ axis 2, and expressive completeness + the transfer instrument are _prerequisites_, not goals.
2. a **completeness taxonomy** (basis / invention / selection) that turns out to carve E7 and E8 at the joints.
3. a **priority** — the transfer-measurement layer is both the top lever and the one workstream that doesn't collide with the concurrent Stitch build.

## The thesis [H]

The research object is **axis 2 — the search-cost graph.** Fix a complete low-level basis; there is then a graph of _costs_ for the loop to climb from atoms to any higher abstraction — how much invention, how much search, to reach each rung. The whole program is: **characterize that graph, understand what reshapes it, and invent novel machinery that improves it** — pushing _past_ existing best practice (DreamCoder/Stitch-grade compression) toward better abstraction-formation dynamics. That last clause is the point of the lab; everything else clears the way to it.

Two things clear the way, and are _prerequisites, not goals_:

- **Expressive completeness** guarantees every target is _definable_, so a failure to invent is a _machinery_ failure, never an expressibility confound. It is the zero-cheating control at the bottom of the ablation ladder — not where we operate.
- **The transfer-measurement layer** supplies the _grade_ (held-out transfer) and the _diagnostic_ (compression↔transfer), so we can tell whether a machinery change actually _helped_ rather than merely _compressed_.

The **completeness taxonomy** below is the vocabulary for saying _where_ on the pipeline a given difficulty lives.

**Goal [C].** Unchanged. The artifact is the _machinery_ — a solver that forms abstractions, plus the search and governance that decide which sub-programs earn a name. The learned library is _observed, not targeted_; ARC score is a gauge, never the objective. Restated against the thesis: the deliverable is _a better search-cost graph, and the machinery that produces it._

**The bootstrap cycle [H].** Unchanged, now with a name for what it does: it is an **automatic floor-raiser**. Solve small-gap tasks → compress into abstractions → abstractions speed up search → bridge a wider gap → repeat. In axis-2 terms, each turn of the wheel _rewrites the search-cost graph from below_, manufacturing the tractability-critical mid-level primitives instead of gifting them.

## The two axes [H]

The abstraction space has **two orthogonal axes**, and conflating them (we did, briefly) hides the phenomenon:

1. **Completeness → a dependency graph.** Can the basis _express_ X _at all_? A partial order: X is expressible once its generative atoms are present. Removing a **generative atom** (`if`) breaks completeness; removing a **derived primitive** (`rot90`) does not — it stays re-derivable. This axis is nearly binary and mostly a precondition.
2. **Grain (low ↔ high) → a search-cost graph.** _Among complete bases_, how expensive is each low→high mapping? Gifting a derived primitive keeps completeness but _shortcuts_ the graph. **This axis is the phenomenon** — the object of study, the thing machinery reshapes.

Axis 1 asks _can it_; axis 2 asks _at what cost_. The research we're most interested in lives on axis 2.

**Expressibility is nearly free [C].** Grids are finite, so every grid→grid function is a lookup table, and `build_grid + read + the L0 control block (if / eq / bool / arithmetic)` is a _universal constructor_ — output at a computed size, each cell an arbitrary decision tree over the input. So completeness is ~one small primitive block away, and provable. But the program expressibility guarantees is the lookup table (DL ≈ the truth-table size): **expressible ≠ compressible ≠ findable.** This is the Kolmogorov point — in any universal basis everything is expressible; the whole game is _length_. So the real target is **low-DL / findable** completeness over the ARC-relevant region — an empirical question about real ARC, not a proof. Expressive completeness is the **zero-cheating control**, not the operating point. "Cheating" **[H]** has a principled handle: the _compositional height_ of the gifted set above the domain-general atoms; the principle _gift control, withhold domain_ draws the line at L0/L1 (control + substrate) vs. L2+ (domain content).

## The completeness taxonomy [H]

"Can it invent X" hides three completenesses:

- **Basis-complete for X** (lever 1) — the atoms can _express_ X.
- **Invention-complete for X** (lever 3, proposer) — the proposer can _surface_ X as a candidate.
  - **E7 is an invention-incompleteness failure**: whole-program antiunify structurally could not surface `mirror_index` (a subterm); frequent-subtree could.
- **Selection-complete for X** (lever 3, governance) — the selector _will mint_ X.
  - **E8 is a selection-incompleteness failure**: the proposer surfaced `mirror_index`, greedy-MDL buried it.

Notes:

- Beneath invention sits a lever-2 precondition — **recurrence**: compression can only invent what _repeats_, so a single-use abstraction is invisible to library learning by construction (this is precisely what per-task descriptive models — Ferré — capture that library learning cannot).
- **capability vs. tractability**: the _capability_ form of each completeness is cheap (a dumb enumerator is trivially invention-complete); each becomes a research object only when bounded by tractability — at which point it collapses into **axis 2**.
- **Selection _correctness_, not completeness [C].** We do **not** want selection-complete = invention-complete.
  - Most invention-complete candidates _should_ be rejected — they're legitimately useless, and a selector that mints them all is broken, not complete.
  - The goal is an **accurate** useful/not-useful verdict; the failure mode we chase is the **false negative** (`mirror_index` — judged useless-by-MDL when it is useful).
  - Selection should be a _strong, accurate filter_, not a permissive one.

## The floor lattice + the floor ⇄ machinery trade-off [H]

There is **no single right floor** — there is a _lattice_ of complete bases ordered by grain, and the **floor-height → tractability mapping is the object of study.** Facts:

- The **lowest complete floor is intractable** for the higher tiers. The E5–E9 beam cliff (depth-2 reflections findable only at beam 128) is the canary; object-level logic at the atomic floor is depth-8+ and hopeless for blind search.
- So higher complete floors _are_ more tractable — gifted derived primitives collapse deep compositions to depth-1 calls. The tractability-critical ones are the high-leverage nodes in the invention graph — **exactly what the bootstrap invents first.** Hand-gifting a mid-level floor is therefore a _control_: does the loop _rediscover_ the primitives you'd have gifted?
- **"How low can we go" = "how low _for a given machinery_."** Floor-height and machinery-quality trade off: dumb search needs a high floor; better machinery tolerates a lower one. So **lowering the floor is the pressure test that surfaces novel machinery** — the research loop _is_: lower the floor → search walls → invent machinery to breach it → lower further. "How low can we go" and "what novel machinery helps" are one question from two ends.

## The experiment family [O]

The concrete expression of the axis-2 focus — the program the maps and the instrument exist to enable. From a complete low-level basis:

- Which abstractions can the loop invent, and how fast — separately for **invention** and **search**?
- Which are **hardest** to invent (dependency depth / DL-gap crossed per generation)?
- Do gifted **mid-level primitives** speed invention — which ones, by how much (the search-cost-graph shortcut they buy)?
- **Ablations** — remove a primitive (or a machinery component); what's still inventable, at what cost?
- **The north star:** _novel machinery / techniques / strategies that improve the search-cost graph_ — and the measured proof that they do.

## The measurement bottleneck [H]

The project is **measurement-bottlenecked, not machinery-bottlenecked.** Three things converge on one missing instrument: (a) the _grade_ is held-out transfer, and it is an IOU (`enablement_transfer` is same-corpus only); (b) the E8 finding is literally _train-DL ≠ reusability_ — the metric we optimize diverges from the thing we want, and the fix (selection-correct governance) is undefinable without a reusability signal; (c) the central open question _does compression predict transfer_ is a correlation we cannot compute until transfer is measured. Building the instrument unblocks all three at once. **Real ARC is the eventual _adjudicator_ this instrument reads — not a substitute for it.** Grading the loop on real ARC without the transfer instrument buys a solve count (a sanity gauge), not a dynamics finding.

**How the taxonomy organizes the work [H].** Measurement _locates_ where selection-completeness fails → selection-correct governance _fixes_ the verdict → library refactoring / Stitch _extends_ invention reach (required for the north star: definition-absorbed and higher-order abstractions a corpus-only proposer can't recover) → basis/ablation science _maps_ axis 2.

## Carried forward — levers, prior art, disciplines

- **Levers [H].** Floor × Curriculum × Machinery, a factorial design moved one at a time (unchanged). The **descriptive vs. transformational** floor axis (07-07's sharpest framing) remains the highest-leverage _empty region_ and the longer-horizon bet; near-term we deepen the _transformational_ floor (it compounds, and the descriptive bet needs the transfer instrument to be evaluable). Completeness is **paradigm-relative** — a move to a descriptive floor restarts both axes.
- **Where we sit [H].** Placement vs. Ferré/MADIL (descriptive + MDL, _no_ cross-task library learning), DreamCoder/Stitch (transformational library learning), CompressARC (per-puzzle MDL, ~20% eval — validation of the north star and its ceiling), the LLM-TTT frontier (off it, deliberately) — unchanged. And the **axis-2 / basis-ablation program is a contribution none of the three families make**: the search-cost _geometry_ of abstraction formation, instrumented against a transfer grade.
- **Disciplines [C].** Targets → observables; optimize compression, **report** transfer, **diagnose** the correlation; a low-floor negative indicts Floor × Machinery, never the floor alone; preserve regression locks, keep determinism, `make check` green; _adopt don't rebuild, maps predict tasks adjudicate_. **New [C]:** the usefulness signal governance consumes is a **train-side proxy**, so held-out transfer stays an _uncontaminated_ grade — never grade the optimizer by its own objective.

## Open [O]

- Does the bootstrap _climb_ — traverse the invention graph from the atomic basis — and where does it wall?
