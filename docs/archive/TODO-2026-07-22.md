## Chosen direction (2026-07-22) — the cfb2ce5a variant program

**Push the real-ARC ladder cfb2ce5a through the full pipeline, then build variants of it.** This single workstream collapses three board items into one, and is the center of gravity from here:

- **Item 14 (real-ARC anchoring)** — cfb2ce5a is the first real-task contact; getting it to certify (or collapse) is the headline.
- **Item 11 (granularity family)** — variants of _one_ task that share a floor and top ARE the granularity family, on a real task instead of a synthetic one. Because they share the top under the same floor, **raw cancels**: variant-vs-variant comparison is fully measured (no estimation, no bounds), and a _single_ raw arm prices RQ1 for every variant at once.
- **Item 10 (grow the set)** — every certifying variant counts toward the growth set.

**Why this and not the alternatives.** Nothing else on the board hits three items with one workstream. The `[L]` items (16, 18) are gated behind _having more ladders_ — which the variants produce. The rest (3, 6, 12, 13) are polish. The synthetic granularity family stays available as the controlled companion if the real one turns up a confound.

**The high-information failure mode is a feature.** The Family-B tension (cheap perception erases real-task gaps) is untested on a real task. If cfb2ce5a certifies with a genuine gap → the program's first real-ARC headline. If it collapses via skip paths → Family-B _confirmed on a real task_, which redirects the real-ARC plan toward laddering perception itself. Either is worth having early.

**Sequence (potentially)**

- v1 (the existing basic, low-rung-cost ladder) through the full staged pipeline — certificate + climb + raw arm — as the baseline column, even though it is suboptimal.
- v2 = merged-rung (fewer, bigger jumps; the theory predicts it wins on end-to-end cost since loop overhead grows with height — al17 was worst at 4.04x — _unless_ a merged jump goes intractable or collapses, which is exactly the granularity knee to locate).
- v3 = a structurally different decomposition, or a HOF/conditional variant if the repetition signal is there.
  - Merged/higher-arity rungs: sleep-probe S-B's arity law + the new `free-params-covary` lint say exactly what demos are needed to mint at the intended arity — checked, not hoped.

### Signals for reaching the unused capabilities (map / fold / if / lambda)

cfb2ce5a's basic ladder uses none of these; the question "how do we find opportunities to?" has mechanical answers worth writing down:

- **map / fold** — repetition in the _solution's own structure_: the same sub-op applied per row/column/tile, or a chain of near-identical steps. `place(place(place(g,…)…)…)` with a varying position is a fold written longhand (al14's cell→row→grid lift was exactly this, first-order).
- **lambda / higher-order rung** — two rungs that are the _same skeleton with a different inner op_ want to become one rung taking that op as an argument (if `add_right` / `add_below` / `place_diagonal` share a shape).
- **if** — only real if a condition takes _both_ truth values across train examples (`if-condition-varies` enforces it); a task that doesn't mode-switch per example would lint as a collapsed conditional, correctly.
- **Guardrails already in place**: `hof-holes-fillable` (item 2) catches the `map`-synthesis config trap; battery E is the price list (point-free ~1.3x cheap; `fold @ lambda-synthesis` 250x — HOF variants need `(depth, pool)`-pair budgets and probe-gating). Caveat both ways: zero ladder-level data exists on HOF/conditional floors, so a HOF variant is riskier _and_ the first data point for a known gap.
