## Premises — settled results this plan builds on (pointers, not re-argument)

1. **The cost law.** Cost-to-first is a step function in the residual depth of the deepest remaining search (~10x per level), with a structural rung/top asymmetry: a rung-serving level pays wake + skip-search-to-exhaustion, the top pays one stoppable search — hence the fitted affordability predicate (rungs ≤ d2, top ≤ d3). Floor breadth is first-order at fixed depth (floor tax up to 95,238x, → 508x after the commutative dedup; the dedup alone moved every real member 2.7–5.1x), and cells censor on depth x pool, not either alone. (EXPERIMENTS 2026-07-27 cut-set + 2026-08-04 pool-wall/canonical-fill entries.)
2. **Both real cohorts are cut-set-exhausted by enumeration** — zero unbuilt affordable members — _under the pre-dedup cost regime_; the screen predates the 2.7–5.1x re-pricing (re-screen: Phase 0).
3. **Ladderability.** Commuting factorisations cannot be laddered by cutting between them (`a740d043`, probe-proved); the predicted admissible non-commuting control has never been built. Task choice is load-bearing.
4. **The learnability column is corpus economics, not learner capability.** Under `TwoPartMDL`, 8 of 11 real-ARC members decline at every `bits_per_primitive` (`b* ≤ 0`): at 2–4 demos per rung no abstraction pays for its definition. The two available selectors are degenerate in opposite directions at this operating point (flat metric cannot say no; TwoPartMDL cannot say yes).
5. **The curriculum decomposition — the sound results are the mode-conditional ones** (evidential frame and scope: the [decomposition notebook](../../../experiments/2026-08-04-curriculum-tax-decomposition/notebook.md)): which class dominates is an accounting-mode artifact (exhaustive → LR ~43%; stop-first → HR ~83%); M ≈ H under exhaustion (class-cost equality); the abstraction benefit (ρ < 1 re-finds) is cashable only under a stop policy; the CR+LR + to-first counterfactual removes a **median ~7.7x per member** (range 1.9–1716x). Store-wide pooled shares ("~80% doomed"; the pooled 9.1x) are **bookkeeping about the store** — compute-weighted, convenience-sampled, defect-including — never findings about ladders.
6. **Comparability walls, all demonstrated:** cohort licenses (task x floor hash), single-generation cells, and — new — accounting-mode stratification for any curriculum-tax read.

## Decisions of record (2026-08-04)

1. **Selector/governance research is PARKED behind the discovery decision.** Reasoning: the components are deliberately under-invested, so their weakness is unsurprising and improving them is engineering, not findings — _until_ the program attempts rung discovery, where proposal + selection without an oracle becomes the system under test. Checked for blocking: nothing in Phases 0–3 reads a selection decision. Consequences: decoy-style distractor controls (al9/al11 spine rebuild) stay out of this plan — neither current selector can make their readout interpretable; the optional lint-time `b*` economics check is built only if a learnability-readout experiment gets scheduled.
2. **No new members of exhausted cohorts, and no guard escalation on censored cells** — the pool-wall analysis showed those gaps are unrunnable, not unrun. New spend goes to new variation only (floors, schedules, families, shapes).
3. **Cross-search-engine claims are out of scope.** A second engine is a machinery program, not an experiment; every result stays labeled engine-relative (unguided bottom-up enumeration, one order). In-scope order-axis changes of the canonical-fill kind are taken opportunistically, locks as arbiter.
4. **Curriculum-tax metrics adopted, with their degeneracies named.** Into the ladder report as views over per-cell records: class shares under both accountings, M vs the climb's CR compute, ρ drift, solution churn — always mode-stratified. Ratio metrics are geometry-laden, so cross-member reads normalize against the closed-form invocation nulls, never raw. μ*HR / c̄_HR are derived constants in this system (exhaustion at the binding budget) — reported as \_checks* (a violation is a runtime skip-detection), not as measurements.
5. **Predict-then-run is the default discipline for every arm in this plan:** the prediction goes in the notebook or `.ladder` header before the run, as the MVE practice established.
6. **The measurement set is completed before any new experimental arm (2026-08-04 amendment).** The decomposition left every named metric in the [tax-math notes](../../abstraction_ladders/2026-07-29_chatgpt%20-%20tax%20math.md) partially measured (residue list: the decomposition notebook). Completing it is Phase 0 item 1 (read-side) plus the `C_optimal` arm folded into Phase 1's schedule factorial (item 6). Scope cap: the read-side completion is ~a session; a piece that balloons (the authored-rung join is the candidate) is cut to Phase 3 rather than allowed to delay the decisive arms. Two pieces expressly NOT pulled forward: the old-solution-under-new-library replay (`G_new_abstraction` — needs replay machinery; lives with RQ-III(b) in Phase 3), and the surprisal-based variants (**permanently N/A** in this system — no probabilistic grammar; closed, not pending).

7. **Artifact discipline (2026-08-05).** A decomposition number that could have been computed from the `.ladder` file and config _before running_ is an artifact of design x settings, not a finding — class counts are arithmetic from geometry (the invocation identities), class cost rules follow from stop mode + mechanism. Findings are (a) **residuals** from that null, or (b) **mechanism laws with stated scope**; every claim carries its varied-channel statement (what was varied, what was held or computed out). Within-member contrasts (settings varied, design held) and license-matched cohort comparisons (design varied, task x floor held) are the sound shapes; store-wide pooled aggregates are bookkeeping, reported only as such. Mechanized by Phase 0 item 5's predicted-class-profile requirement.

## Research questions

- **RQ-I — Is the affordability limit a cost bound wearing a depth costume?** The predicate's own mechanism says the real limit is "each level's exhaustion fits the guard" = f(breadth, pool, depth). _Method:_ floor-breadth variants of 2–3 existing members (leaner and fatter), plus a pool-aware static predictor fitted on the existing run store (no new N; the naive forecaster's failure mode — pool saturation — is located). _Registered prediction:_ on a leaner floor, d3 rung levels become affordable; the curve's riser moves where the census forecasts.
- **RQ-II — Is rung count epiphenomenal given (depth schedule, entry profile)?** _Method:_ two synthetic arms — a **gratuitous rung** (schedule fixed, count +1) and a **schedule move** (count fixed, one level d2 → d3). _Registered predictions:_ the first moves cost only by the entry's vocabulary tax plus one climb iteration (percent-scale); the second is a ~10x step, positioned per the wake/skip asymmetry.
- **RQ-III — The curriculum-tax program, as a schedule x adaptation factorial.** Cells: **full-learned** (exists — the recorded climbs); **CR-only-learned** = `C_optimal` proper, the missing denominator for `tau`/`M_full` as defined in the tax-math notes, _and_ the trajectory-invariance check (_prediction:_ mints identical to `full`; a difference is a finding, and would mean the frozen cell is required for a clean denominator); **CR+LR-learned** — never attempt not-yet-reachable tasks, keep re-searching solved ones (_registered prediction:_ mints byte-identical to `full` since sleep consumes solutions only, savings per the decomposition table, 1.9x–33x — a confirmation makes "HR is deadweight, LR is the evidence" a measured statement and prices the curriculum-order prior); **full-frozen** — the full schedule against the chain's per-level libraries, learning off, the cell that splits loop overhead into direct tax vs learning effect (_prediction:_ learning effect ≈ 0 on certified members; also hosts the old-solution replay from the metric residue).
- **RQ-IV — The ladderability control.** The synthetic non-commuting twin of `a740d043` (recolour depends on the crop). _Registered prediction:_ admissible — the law's contrapositive.
- **RQ-V — Out-of-family generality.** One new real task family, screened at selection time by RQ-IV's law and priced by the breadth census before authoring. Every cost-law claim currently carries "on two-halves geometry" silently.
- _Stretch, behind the above:_ the rung-**type** family (does a rung's cost reduce to its signature's argument-pool geometry — the 404x finding as a design law); a tall-tower synthetic family (R = 2..8) for the tax-distance curve and the quadratic invocation regime.
