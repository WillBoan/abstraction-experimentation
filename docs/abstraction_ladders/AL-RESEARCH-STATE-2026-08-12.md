## Premises narrowed or retired

- Search-cost observations are configuration-relative. The evidence does not license universal residual-depth, arity, rung-count, pool, loop-overhead, or cut-set closure laws.
- The E8 mirror-index result is a historically traceable proposal-set contrast: corpus, MDL selector, and consumer were held fixed while proposals changed. It does not establish a general compression-versus-reusability law.
- Raw first-order Stitch output in that line was arity-invalid for the unary AE consumer. Only the valid definition-refactoring and higher-order probes remain interpretable.
- The real-task evidence covers three related tasks and held-out instances of authored competences, not cross-competence transfer.
- The curriculum decomposition is reconstructed same-trace accounting over 21 authored climbs. It does not replay an earlier stop policy, establish causal “deadweight,” or treat 439 nested rows as independent trajectories.
- Historical artifacts do not acquire modern `RunSpec` provenance through later documentation.

See the current [reviewed synthesis](../../EXPERIMENTS.md) for the evidence and material corrections.

## Current planning state

Plausible future directions include:

- better-isolated search-cost experiments;
- new task and floor families;
- a discovery-shaped floor-plus-Top benchmark with no prescribed rungs;

None is selected by this document. If experimental work resumes, it should begin with a new dated, prediction-first plan that declares the estimand, unit of analysis, provenance standard, stopping rule, and interpretation limits before execution.
