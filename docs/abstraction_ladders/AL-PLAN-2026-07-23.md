## Corrections of record

Carried forward from the 2026-07-22 plan (its #1-#6 stand, as amended there), plus:

7. **Search-cost estimation failed, across the board.** We tried to estimate three things and all three came back with error margins too wide to use:
   - **Raw ladder search cost** — the extrapolated bracket contained the measured truth in **2 of 10 cells** at gaps of 1-2 rounds; the batch's real gaps are 4-51 rounds, where the bracket spans up to 36 orders of magnitude ([experiments/2026-07-23-estimator-validation/](../../experiments/2026-07-23-estimator-validation/)).
   - **A rung's cost as a prediction (without running it)** — the forecaster's one-round-ahead projection ranged 0.04x-1.24x across floors (25x under on two of five). Its stronger backtest numbers were near-interpolation on shallow cells and do not generalize.
   - **`b_eff`** — estimates fitted on early rounds do not hold: the ratio accelerates in the growth regime, collapses at semantic saturation (al2's finite D4 group), and drifts with pool size. An overall `b_eff` cannot be estimated well from a prefix of rounds.

   What survives, and what the program now runs on: **actual measurements** (rung costs when cells are run — exact, no estimate needed), **censored lower bounds** (a raw arm that spends without solving proves raw cost exceeds the spend), and **comparisons where raw cancels** (A/B ladders on the same floor+top, the loop-overhead factor, break-even horizon). The one scoped-and-validated predictive fact: round-1 composition counts are exact arithmetic from the typed leaf census (24/24 cells) — useful for attribution and guards, not a substitute for measurement. Estimation itself is **deferred research, not abandoned** — a structural model that handles dedup/saturation would reopen forecast-guided design, but nothing may depend on one until it survives a validation like 2026-07-23's.

## Decisions of record (2026-07-23)

1. **RQ1 is measured-or-bounded; the extrapolated estimate is retired from results.** Definition per design doc §4: `amortization ratio = raw cost / (laddered cost + learning cost)`, so ratio > 1 = the ladder won. The mechanism:

   > Run the raw arm (top task under the Floor) deliberately: `depth_limit` raised toward `d_raw`, pool freed, saturation-checked, with guard = **K x the measured laddered cost** (K >= 1, chosen per ladder). Either it **solves** — RQ1 is a _measured_ ratio (<= K) — or it **censors** — RQ1 is a _proven lower bound_: ratio >= K.

   Soundness conditions, both mandatory: the raw arm must not saturate (a pool-starved search idles; its spend stops being evidence — the probe's saturation flag detects this), and every bound is reported with its `(depth_limit, max_pool, spend)` conditions. Until the wake/sleep conversion weight `w` is calibrated (Phase 2), the denominator is the wake-side laddered cost, stated as such. `_estimate_raw_cost` survives only as an advisory diagnostic carrying its measured 2/10 caveats — never in a headline.

2. **Nine ladders retired** — the lint-failers whose failure is a defect: al3, al4, al5, al6, al7, al9, al11, al13, al14. Terminal: no fix-and-rerun under these IDs (rebuilds, e.g. al9/al11 on al17's spine, get new IDs). Rows and `.ladder` files persist — the rejections are findings and the files are lint-lock fixtures. **Not retired**: al8 (kept as the head-to-head cost arm, not a ladder), al10/al12 (controls that fail by design and function correctly).

3. **al2 is retired as the calibration instrument** (its finite-group floor has no growth regime to validate against) — though it remains an admitted, runnable ladder.

## The pipeline (unchanged)

Author/load (`.ladder`, via `arc-lab new-ladder`) → **lint** (~8s batch; includes the round-1 breadth census) → **rung probe** → **oracle chain + certificate** → **LEARN climb + reports**. Gate layering: LADDER-CHECKS; the instrument contracts and the failure→response table are [LADDER-PROCESS-2026-07-26.md](LADDER-PROCESS-2026-07-26.md).

> ⚠ The probe's cost was quoted here as **~1s/rung**, which silently assumed the default 50k guard and a lean floor. It tracks the enumeration it drives: seconds on a D4 floor, **minutes on a fat one** (`dae9d2b5-halves-union` r_1 is ~21M considered per cell). Cells are cached since 2026-07-26, so the cost is paid once — but lint, which is genuinely ~1s and static, usually explains an expensive cell before it is ever run.

## Phase 1 — pipeline

1. **DONE (2026-07-23).** Staged `run_ladder` — `run_ladder_chain` (lint + oracle chain) runs first, `certify` gates, and the climb stage (LEARN + off-chain, both moved there) runs only for admitted ladders. A rejected ladder costs exactly its oracle chain; its report carries everything stage 1 measures with the climb sections marked absent-not-zero (`climb_executed: false`). Escape hatch for control arms whose measurement IS the climb: `--climb-rejected`. Locked by fast al10 tests (rejected → no learn/off-chain runs; forced → both).
2. **DONE (2026-07-23).** Raw-arm spend policy — decision 1's K-rule wired in: `run_raw_arm` (climb stage; refuses to run unguarded) sizes the guard as `ceil(K x chain-measured laddered / |top tasks|)`, budget `depth_limit = min_depth_limit(unfolded top)`, pool freed to 200k, `solution_limit=1 immediate` (a solving arm's spend is cost-to-first, so measured ratios conservatively understate the win). The report's `cost.raw_arm` is RQ1's authority (`measured` / `lower-bound` / `lower-bound (UNSOUND: pool-saturated)` — the saturation check withdraws starved bounds); the extrapolated estimate renders as ADVISORY with its 2/10 record. CLI `--raw-arm-k` (default 10). **First measured RQ1 number: al1 = 3.59x** — the arm _solved_ (192,865 to first vs laddered 53,711), so the retired estimate's 78x–530x was ~22x–147x high on the one ladder it was quoted for.
3. **DONE (2026-07-23).** Wake task-scheduling param — `LearnSpec.wake_schedule` (`full` / `skip-solved` / `curriculum`) + explicit `curriculum` task-id groups, all in run identity (differing schedules never share a `run_id`). Non-`full` schedules imply carrying; iterations past the last curriculum group fall back to the full corpus. Arm-labeled everywhere it could mislead: every wake trace row, the learn summary, and a banner in the ladder report when a climb ran non-`full` (its end-to-end cost, loop overhead, and what sleep saw are assisted measurements). The executor never infers a schedule from task metadata — groups are explicit ids, so the blindness seam holds. Locked by fast loop tests (skip-solved re-searches nothing solved; curriculum wakes exactly its group).

6. **DONE (2026-07-23), answered for the clean set: the gap is exactly zero.** All 24 wake iterations across the 8 admitted ladders cost byte-identical to their oracle columns ([experiments/2026-07-23-loop-overhead/](../../experiments/2026-07-23-loop-overhead/notebook.md)) — sleep's mints are cost-equivalent to the gifted rungs when recovery is perfect, which on the admitted set it always is. A NONZERO gap needs a deliberately imperfect climb — folded into item 5, where `skip-solved`'s witnessed stale-input specializations (al1's frozen `map_color(#0,3,4)`; al17's duplicate of frame1) are the first concrete mechanism.
