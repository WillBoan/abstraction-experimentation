## Corrections of record

Conclusions the batch + alignment sessions established, superseding earlier working assumptions:

1. **Jump cost, not `d_raw`, is the load-bearing interestingness axis** — and jump cost is not depth alone: per-round cost = Σ over primitives of Π over argument slots of type-population sizes, so depth sets the number of multiplications while arity, constants and type populations set the multiplicands (measured: params dominate — +2.5% vocabulary tax param-free vs +282% for a 2-param rung; `b_eff` 15 → 110 under arity 3). "Unlock deep jumps" means "unlock high-cost jumps, with a forecaster attributing which factor eats the budget." — _sharpened 2026-07-22 by the micro-probes (Phase 1 item 7):_ the law is **exact**, not a model (predicted round-1 == actual, 24/24 cells), and **arity is not itself a driver** — it is only the number of factors in the product. One wide slot beats two narrow ones: arity 4 with a COLOR slot outcosts arity 5 with INT slots. Reason in `PROD(type populations)`.
2. **The depth-2/3 jump ceiling was a design habit, never a measured limit** — _amended 2026-07-22 by the frontier sweep (Phase 1 item 6):_ true, but incomplete. Depth is cheap at the batch's pool settings **because `max_pool` starves it** — rounds past the cap compose zero candidates, so raising `depth_limit` alone is both free and useless. The load-bearing quantity is the **`(depth_limit, max_pool)` pair**; a jump should be described as "depth 3 at pool 20,000", never "depth 3". Measured throughput ~19–71k candidates/s.
3. **Collapse = the intended template failing to be the cost-minimal representative of its signature class.** The enumerator dedupes by behavior on train examples and keeps the cheapest representative — collapse is the engine working as designed. The three families are three mechanisms: operator algebra (doubling, D4 cancellation), an expressive floor (Family-B), parameter/spec degeneracy (literal collapse, collisions). The first and third now have static checks (`rewrite-shallow`, `constant-subterm`); the empirical certificate remains the authority.
4. **Laws are reasoned, constants are measured; ladder batches validate, micro-probes discover.** Single-factor attribution needs one rung cell with one change (± `if` summoner, constant-domain width, param count, arity trade), not cross-ladder comparison — the batch's al4-proposer and shared-al5-floor confounds are the cautionary examples.
5. **Any deep template can be bisected into depth-2 jumps; the obstruction is never syntax.** "Abstraction X needs jump depth > 3" means every cut of X fails demonstrability, non-collapse, or load-bearing-ness (**bisection failure** — design doc §5.1 already mandates logging these; none logged yet). Characterizing bisection failure ("what makes an abstraction atomic?") is a first-class research question.
6. **`considered_limit` now lives in the reference config** (settled by the `.ladder` migration): guards are ladder identity, censoring is a designed outcome, and future guards should come from forecasts, not round numbers.

## The pipeline

Author/load (`.ladder`, line-numbered errors) → **lint** (`arc-lab lint-ladder`; static + evaluation-backed + equational checks, ~8s for the batch) → **rung probe** (planned, Phase 0) → **oracle chain + certificate** (admission — to be split so no rejected ladder pays for a climb) → **LEARN climb + reports**.

## Phase 0 — instruments

1. **DONE (2026-07-22).** Lint hardening: call-site-aggregated `free-param-varies`; exercised-set completeness (`If`/`PrimRef`); `constant-subterm` + `if-condition-varies` (evaluation-backed); `rewrite-shallow` (`analysis/equations.py` + `analysis/rewrite.py`); `policy_constants`; `BRANCHING_ENTRY` export; memoized `unfold_program`. Batch effect: 11/20 ladders now fail lint, statically confirming the certificate's verdicts with printed witnesses (see LADDER-CHECKS "What the checks currently find").
2. **DONE (2026-07-22).** Rung probe — `ladders/probe.py` + `arc-lab probe-ladder`: wake (retained vs intended: `as-intended`/`collapsed`/`collision`/`alternative`/`unsolved`/`censored`), skip, sleep (graded on what wake retained, arity checked), forecast. **Closes the §6.4 task-collision gap.** Needed deterministic position-separating probe grids to have any discriminating power — al14's own grids confound the two cells its known collision reads, in all 16 (see LADDER-CHECKS Layer 2b). Sound ladders probe clean in ~1s; the batch sweep reproduces the certificate's structural verdicts rung-locally, with programs printed.
3. **DONE (2026-07-22).** Forecaster — `execution/forecast_cost.py`, a SIBLING of `estimate_cost.py` rather than an extension (a ceiling must never under-count; a forecast should be close): typed per-type census, the new-layer factor (`estimate_cost`'s own named "unbuilt refinement"), measured survival via `survival_from(stats)`, and dominant-factor attribution per round. **Backtested on 336 completed rounds across 54 rung cells** (`experiments/2026-07-22-rung-probe/artifacts/forecast_backtest.py`): geomean predicted/actual **21.6x → 1.07x**, within-2x **60% → 97%**. Wired into the probe as "what would one more round of depth cost here?", calibrated on the cell's own funnel. Two results worth carrying: the backtest caught a zero-growth bug that would have silently returned 0 on deep cells, and **carrying an early survival rate forward over-predicts** (dedup rises with pool size), so the deepest observed rate is the one to project.
4. **Wake task-scheduling param** (`full` / `skip-solved` / `oracle-curriculum`) — a `LearnSpec` param in run identity, arm-labeled per design doc §3.7 (curriculum = oracle-schedule arm; it also changes what sleep sees, so recovery claims need honest cells too). Default cheap modes for screening; `full` for headline RQ1 / loop-overhead cells. MACHINERY.md F4 row exists.
5. **Staged `run_ladder`** — chain + certificate before the climb; off-chain moves to the climb stage.

## Phase 1 — measurements

6. **DONE (2026-07-22).** Tractability frontier sweep — `cost_L(d)` on four representative floors at depth 2–5 and `max_pool` 400 vs 20,000 ([experiments/2026-07-22-tractability-frontier/](../../experiments/2026-07-22-tractability-frontier/)). **Result: depth alone is not the cost driver — the `(depth_limit, max_pool)` PAIR is.** At the batch's pool settings depth 4–5 costs seconds, but the extra rounds compose _zero_ candidates (pool starvation, not exploration); free the pool and the same depth-3 cell goes 170,627 → 3,712,560 (22x from the pool knob alone). The cell floor stays the outlier (depth 3 blew a 5M guard by itself). Consequences for this plan: budgets are stated as pairs from here on; correction #2 below is amended — the depth ceiling was never binding, _and_ relaxing it alone buys nothing.
7. **DONE (2026-07-22).** Micro-probe batteries — six single-factor batteries on a deliberately unsolvable floor ([experiments/2026-07-22-micro-probes/](../../experiments/2026-07-22-micro-probes/)). **Result: arity is not the cost driver — `PROD(type populations)` is.** Arity 4 with a COLOR slot costs _more_ than arity 5 with INT slots (632,944 vs 591,302 at depth 3); at arity pinned to 3, moving only the slot types spans 404x (`translate` 1,012 · `pad` 41,596 · `map_color` 408,612). The product law is **exact** (predicted round-1 == actual, 24/24 cells), so a rung's cost is computable from its floor's signature. Other constants measured: constant policy 1.5x (harvest) vs 46x (enumerate) — and the enumerate branch is also what manufactures literal collapse; if-tax 2.0–7.1x; two arity-2 primitives beat one arity-3 for the same expressible set (0.37x). HOF tax spans 1.0x–250x by HOF, and **`map @ lambda-synthesis` is a silent no-op under every preset** (`unpinned_type_var_mode='reject'` skips free hole return types) — Phase 2's HOF cells must set it deliberately. The batteries also found and fixed two forecaster bugs on floors no ladder builds (the backtest is byte-identical before/after), which is correction #4 working as stated.

   _Design rules of record from this:_ reason in `PROD(type populations)` per slot, never arity or depth; prefer `harvest-from-instance` to `finite-enumerate` for parameterised rungs; split parameters across rungs rather than widening a slot.

8. **DONE (2026-07-23).** Estimator validation — done as a 4-floor sweep, not a calibration ladder ([experiments/2026-07-23-estimator-validation/](../../experiments/2026-07-23-estimator-validation/)). **al2 is retired as the calibration instrument**: its floor generates the 8-element D4 group, so its composed counts are flat and no growth fit can be validated on it. **Result: the estimator's bracket contained the measured truth in 2 of 10 cells**, failing in BOTH directions — accelerating-growth floors under-project on both fits (82x–269x under at a 2-round gap), finite-space floors over-project (up to 3.71x). `forecast_cost` is ~30x tighter overall (median 1.00x, range 0.04x–1.24x) but is 25x under on al1's own floor, so it is not a drop-in replacement and nothing was swapped. **Consequence: RQ1 ratios carry ~3 orders of magnitude of uncertainty in an unknown direction** — al1's 78x–530x is not merely unvalidated, it comes from a method that brackets correctly on al1's floor and is wrong by 269x elsewhere. Code change confined to making the report honest about itself (docstring warning, measured caveats in every report, a test pinning them).

   _Open decision, promoted out of this item:_ **RQ1 needs redefining, not a better fit.** The strongest candidate is **marginal rung value** (cost of layer _i_ under `L_{i-1}` vs under `L_i`) — measured on both sides, no extrapolation, already computed by the report. Alternatives: report RQ1 as a floor with an error band, or restrict extrapolation to 1-round gaps. This changes what the AL program claims, so it is a research decision.

## Phase 2 — new science

9. **Granularity family** — same floor/top/spine cut at heights 2/3/5, corpus factored so shared lower-level cells share `(Config, Corpus)` (cache hits); measures total cost vs decomposition granularity — the "optimal learning path" question made empirical. Semantic meaningfulness is measured only against corpora not hand-picked around the ladder (deferred to real-ARC anchoring).
10. **Deep-jump ladders** — depth-3/4 jumps, forecast-guarded, pool-saturated where needed with probe-checked reachability.
11. **Family falsification probes** — perceived-index al14 rebuild (family 3 predicts it survives); expensive-perceiver al5 variant (family 2 predicts the gap returns); first HOF-floor and conditional-floor cells (machinery supports both; zero data exists). The al7 plain-concat control tower is the model.
12. **Then**: generate-and-certify ladder generation (the probe as the generator's inner-loop verifier), real-ARC anchoring — noting the Family-B tension: real tasks are perception-heavy and cheap perception erases gaps, so real-ARC ladders likely need perception itself laddered.
